The menubar's dropdowns are laid out in flow rather than floating under their triggers: EVG has no overlay layer yet, so a menu cannot paint above the page. That is the next piece of work in gallery/ui/PLAN.md, and it is the reason the page shows one menu at a time. The toolbar's italic and underlined buttons are missing for a different reason: EVG has no font-style and no text-decoration.
