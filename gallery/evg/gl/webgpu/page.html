The same display lists through both painters, in one page. Milliseconds, median of the repeats. build is list → GPU-ready buffers, uploaded and drawn once; redraw is the frame kept and only the layer shift moved, which is what a scroll costs; bundle is the WebGPU-only replay of a recorded frame. A ratio above 1 means WebGPU is that many times faster.
