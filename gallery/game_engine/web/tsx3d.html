Ranger Three — the Three.js cube, on the GPU

The canonical Three.js rotating-cube example runs 1:1, unmodified (import * as THREE from 'three' and all) in Ranger's .tsx interpreter, against a thin three.tsx façade — and renders with real WebGL. No three.js JavaScript: the 3D engine is a Ranger clone, the same source that compiles to native OpenGL.
